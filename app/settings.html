Privacy Policy

How we handle and secure your campus data.

Effective Date: September 2026
Platform: Domasi Hub (Campus Utility Platform)

Domasi Hub ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you access our web and mobile application designed for students and service providers at Domasi College of Education.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a reliable campus utility experience, we collect specific categories of information:

  • Account Credentials: Email addresses and secure hashed passwords processed via Supabase authentication to verify student and seller accounts.
  • Profile & Service Data: Information provided voluntarily by campus sellers or users when setting up profiles, uploading campus service listings, or posting utility items.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Device information, app version data, local storage states (such as theme preferences and welcome screen flags), and connection logs required to maintain application performance and stability.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your information strictly for lawful and operational objectives related to campus life:

  • Authenticating user sessions and safeguarding account security.
  • Powering the dedicated seller portal to manage service uploads, listings, and student interactions.
  • Enforcing application logic, such as remembering your theme choices and whether you have completed the initial onboarding flow.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures—including encrypted database connections via PostgreSQL/Supabase, secure token handling, and strict password hashing protocols—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identifiable information to third parties. Data is only shared with trusted infrastructure providers (such as secure cloud and database hosts) strictly bound by confidentiality obligations to keep your platform data operational and safe.

5. User Rights & Data Control

You retain full control over your account. You can update your profile details, modify your account security credentials (such as changing your password) through the settings interface, or terminate your usage by signing out of the platform at any time.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform upgrades or legal compliance changes. Continued use of Domasi Hub after modifications constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
