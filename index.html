About Domasi Hub

One digital campus companion.

Domasi Hub brings students, educators, vendors, and service providers into a shared campus operating system for discovery, communication, and delivery.

Domasi Hub is designed to help students find what they need quickly: accommodation ideas, academic resources, marketplace deals, service contacts, printing support, and student notices from the campus community.

The app gives every user a smarter way to move through university life by combining resource discovery, content publishing, partner services, and personalized campus updates in a single mobile-first experience.
